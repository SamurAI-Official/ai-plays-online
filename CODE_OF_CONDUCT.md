# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in all of its spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and code review.

## In short

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

**Expected:** being kind and patient with people who are learning, giving and accepting constructive feedback, focusing
on what is best for the project and its users, and taking responsibility for mistakes.

**Not acceptable:** sexualised language or attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, public or
private harassment, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption.

## Scope, and one thing specific to this project

The Covenant applies to project spaces and to anyone representing the project in public.

This project is about running **games you dumped yourself**. Asking for, offering or linking ROMs, BIOS or firmware is
out of bounds here, as is pressuring anyone to break the laws of their country to try the app.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers — privately, through
**Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository if you would rather not be seen doing it in public
(<!-- TODO: replace with the address you want to be reached at, e.g. conduct@… --> the maintainers are also reachable
through the organisation's GitHub profile). Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential.

Responses follow the Covenant's ladder: a private correction, then a warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent
ban — proportional to the behaviour and its impact, with an explanation to the person concerned, and the right to
appeal to a different maintainer.
